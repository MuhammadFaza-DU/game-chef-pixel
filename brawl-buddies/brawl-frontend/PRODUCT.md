# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players who open a browser-based arcade action cooking game on desktop or mobile and want to get into a playful fight quickly.

## Product Purpose

Brawl Buddies: Chaos Kitchen is a chaotic cooking brawler where the menu should make the player feel they are entering a loud, funny kitchen fight, not a generic neon arcade template.

## Brand Personality

Messy, punchy, cartoon. The game should feel like a kitchen where ingredients, orders, pans, and rivals are already flying before the player presses start.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark neon menus, empty silhouette heroes, stock pixel glow, decorative gradients without game context, and UI that looks like a template rather than a specific cooking brawler.

## Design Principles

Show the kitchen fight immediately.
Make every decorative element explain the game world.
Keep controls and primary actions readable before adding flair.
Use cartoon arcade detail over abstract atmosphere.
Preserve responsive playability on desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, clear focus states, reduced-motion fallbacks, and responsive layouts that do not hide core actions.
